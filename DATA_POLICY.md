# Data Policy

The project publishes public-interest summaries and source links, not sensitive operational data. We do not publish:

- Confidential license-plate scan records
- Individual vehicle histories
- Personal tracking information
- Unlawfully obtained information
- Confidential law-enforcement data

We do not accept submissions that expose private people, enable harassment, or disclose confidential records. Public sources are reviewed for context and provenance before a factual record is added.

The project does not support vandalism, destruction, interference with equipment, harassment, or unlawful activity. It does not provide features specifically intended to facilitate evasion of law enforcement.

When information is unavailable, the application uses “Not publicly documented” or leaves the field empty. Fictional development fixtures are labeled as such and must not be mistaken for public records.
